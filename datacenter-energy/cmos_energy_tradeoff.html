Beamer Model Connections & Timing Rules

1. Propagation Delay ($t_{\text{delay}}$) $t_{\text{delay}} \propto \frac{C \cdot V}{(V - V_{\text{th}})^\alpha}$. Lowering supply voltage $V$ reduces signal drive current, drastically increasing transistor propagation delay.
2. Timing Violation ($f > f_{\text{max}}$) If $f > \frac{1}{t_{\text{delay}}}$, signals do not stabilize before the clock sampling edge, triggering setup time violations and calculation errors.
3. Energy Trade-off Rule $E_{\text{dyn}} = \alpha C V^2 N$ is independent of $f$. Lowering $f$ saves power, but increases execution time $T_{\text{exec}}$, causing static leakage energy ($V I_{\text{leak}} T_{\text{exec}}$) to rise!
