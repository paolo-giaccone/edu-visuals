Key Takeaways: Why Profile Shape Dictates Optimal Policy

Cubic (DVFS) $\rightarrow$ Load Balancing Wins Because $P(L) \propto L^3$ is strictly convex (super-linear), splitting load equally minimizes total power due to Jensen's Inequality ($f(\text{avg}) < \text{avg}(f)$). Operating 4 servers at 45% load consumes far less power than 2 at 90%.
Linear / Step with High $P_{\text{idle}}$ $\rightarrow$ Consolidation Wins When idle power $P_{\text{idle}}$ is high, spreading work turns on all 4 servers, paying $4 \times P_{\text{idle}}$. Packing work into 2 fully loaded servers lets 2 servers power down or enter deep sleep ($0\text{W}$).
